# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML pages for previews (inline SVG, GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs); production site is Express 4 + EJS + MySQL with a lead inbox at /admin.

## Users
Owners of small and mid-size businesses (shops, restaurants, clinics, salons, trades, online sellers) in North America, the UK and Europe, the Gulf and Australia, plus English-speaking owners in Bangladesh. Busy, not technical, often running the business from a phone. They have usually been let down before by a cheap template, a relative, or a slow, expensive agency.

## Product Purpose
Click & Grow builds websites, online shops, Google and SEO setups, ads, apps and branding for those owners, remotely. The homepage exists to get a quote request, a free website check-up request or a WhatsApp message.

## Positioning
A small remote team that makes a small business look as trusted as a big one, with a clear quote agreed before work starts. Confident and funny, in the spirit of artattackk.com; the jokes land on bad options and office life, never on the visitor.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from search, social posts and referrals, mostly on phones, often in a spare minute between customers. They decide fast and leave if the page feels slow or salesy.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Confirmed offers (the only claims allowed): free website check-up; free logo with every website; free Google and SEO setup; free support after launch; pay in parts; quote agreed upfront; a limited number of projects each month.
- Placeholders until the owner confirms: prices, monthly slot numbers, support length, reply times, delivery timelines.
- Contact: WhatsApp and phone +880 1732-508485; the team is based in Dhaka and works remotely.
- Payment: card, bank transfer or PayPal; bKash or Nagad for clients in Bangladesh (mentioned only in the FAQ).

## Brand Commitments
Name: Click & Grow. Colours: green #006A4E, yellow #FFC72C, ink #1C1A17, paper #FFFBF2. Logo: a cursor in a rounded green square. Voice: plain, dry, cheeky; no em or en dashes; short lines.

## Evidence on Hand
No client logos, testimonials or case-study numbers yet. Demo projects must be labelled as demos. One research citation in use: Lindgaard et al. (2006) on 50 ms first impressions.

## Product Principles
- Show it, don't explain it: one picture per idea, few words.
- Every claim true; placeholders marked.
- Never hold the visitor's scroll.
- Easy first step: the free check-up sits beside every quote button.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, every animation has a reduced-motion end state, works at 390 px wide, form errors named in text with a focusable summary, no emoji standing in for icons.
