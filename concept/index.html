How it works

Our process, from napkin sketch to live site.

Four stages, each with something you can see and click. You sign off before we move on, so there are no surprises at the end.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    A call, a short brief and paper sketches of the pages that matter most.

    week 1
  2. 02

    Wireframe

    Clickable grey-box pages to test the flow before a single colour is picked.

    week 2
  3. 03

    Design

    Full visual design for desktop and phone, with your real words and products.

    weeks 3–4
  4. 04

    Launch

    Built, tested on real phones, launched, then looked after on a care plan.

    week 5 onward
