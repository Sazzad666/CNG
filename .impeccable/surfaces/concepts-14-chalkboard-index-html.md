# Concept 14: chalkboard homepage

Scope: whole homepage concept, Persuade mode. Mobile first; most visitors are shop owners on a phone in a spare minute.
Job: make them believe a Click & Grow site keeps selling after they close, then get a quote request or a WhatsApp message.
Proof and content: the facts, prices, packs, steps and FAQ already used in concept 09 (sample prices, labelled as samples). No invented clients or reviews.
Constraints: brand green #006A4E, yellow #FFC72C, ink #1C1A17, paper #FFFBF2; no em or en dashes in copy; calm motion (the user found concept 09's hero too busy).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shopkeeper's own A-frame chalkboard, the board every corner shop props outside its door. It refuses the agency default of a headline beside a device mockup and the busy illustrated street of concept 09.
OWN-WORLD: Slate green-black board ground with a worn wooden frame, chalk lettering in white, brand yellow and soft green chalk, smudged eraser marks, chalk-drawn icons and arrows, hand-ruled menu lines, a cream paper order slip as the one non-chalk surface. Type: a chalky hand display face plus a plain readable body face.
STORY: The visitor reads "Shop's shut. Website's open.", watches tonight's orders tally up on the board while a chalk plant grows, understands a Click & Grow website sells overnight, scans the services written like a specials board, and leaves a quote request on the board itself.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: the board fills the screen inside its wooden frame; chalked headline top, a tally of tonight's orders with a running total and a small chalk plant beside it, then a working line "Your shop's name" with a Get my quote button and a WhatsApp link. Desktop: the same board wider, headline and form left, tally and plant right.
FORM: Market chalkboard A-frame, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:aad08d4f0f8b4570
SIGNATURE: the headline underline, the pot and the tally frame draw themselves once on arrival; then one chalk tally mark every two seconds, a leaf for every second order and a flower on the last one, fourteen marks in all, then everything rests.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
