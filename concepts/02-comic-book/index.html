Behind the panels · concept notes

Why a comic?

The idea

Artattackk decorates its pages with doodles. We use art to tell one story: a client with a quiet website meets the crew, and three months later she can’t keep up with orders. Visitors see themselves in Mira, so the pitch makes itself.

Copy rules

  • Words live in bubbles and captions. Max 12 per bubble.
  • Characters talk like people. No one says “solutions”.
  • Every section has one headline, one sub, one thing to do.
  • We deliver innovative digital solutions.
  • Slow sites lose sales. So I don’t build slow sites.

UX choices

  • “Skip to the last page” in the header: the quote form is one click away from anywhere.
  • A page counter shows how far through the story you are.
  • Process is a page you flip, with arrow keys, not a wall of text.
  • The contact form is one sentence with four blanks.
  • Everything still reads with motion turned off.
