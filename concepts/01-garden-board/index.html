Click & Grow

The big idea

Every click plants something.

An IT company site that shows growth instead of talking about it. The cursor is a seed, every section is a stage of growth, and every hover makes something sprout. Few words, lots to watch.

The story

One metaphor, the whole way down.

Artattackk wins with personality. We keep that energy but swap random doodles for one story the visitor plays through: plant, grow, bloom.

Homepage storyboard

Five scroll chapters, five growth stages.

Each chapter has one headline, one line of copy and one thing to play with. Try the previews: hover, tap and drag.

Sunlight

Chapter 4 · Why us

Meet Sprig. Sprig hates boring websites.

Our mascot replaces paragraphs of “why us”. Four short promises float around Sprig and grow toward your cursor.

Moves

Built in

Harvest

The look

Riso garden, not grey sketchbook.

Artattackk draws in grey pencil. We draw in thick ink with bright flat fills printed slightly off the line, like a risograph poster. Same hand-made warmth, our own colour.

Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, chunky and a bit wonky. Figtree for the few sentences we keep. Caveat for handwritten notes in the margins.

notes like this, sparingly ✿

  • 3px ink lines, round caps, everywhere
  • Fills sit a hair off the line (the riso wobble)
  • Hard offset shadows, no soft blur
  • No stock photos, no gradients, no 3D blobs

Voice

Talk like a friend who codes.

Short, warm, a little cheeky. If a line needs a second sentence, the animation should be doing that job instead.
