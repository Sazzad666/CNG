    Troubleshooting

    Questions, including the awkward ones.

    ERR_01Will a website really fix slow business?

    A website can't sell something nobody wants. What it does is make sure the people already looking for you can find you, trust you and buy without waiting on you. That's usually where the orders go missing.

    ERR_02You're in Bangladesh. Can you work with me in London, Dubai or New York?

    Yes. Most of our work is remote. We schedule calls in your time zone and invoice in USD, GBP, EUR or AED.

    ERR_03What's in the free website check-up?

    We look at your website or social page and send you a short list of what's costing you customers. It's free, and you don't have to hire us afterwards.

    ERR_04Can I pay in parts?

    Yes. We split the payment into parts and agree the split with you before we start.

    ERR_05Will the price go up halfway through?

    No. The quote you agree is the quote you pay. If you ask for something new later, we quote that separately and you decide.

    ERR_06I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run your website. We show you how.

    ERR_07How long does it take?

    Most websites take 7 to 21 days. Online shops and apps take longer. You get the timeline with your quote.

    ERR_08Do you take bKash?

    Yes, along with cards and bank transfer. Clients abroad usually pay by card or wire.
